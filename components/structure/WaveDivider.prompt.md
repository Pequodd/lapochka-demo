The wavy section boundary. Depth in this system comes from overlapping layers and these waves — never from shadows.

```jsx
<WaveDivider variant={2} above="var(--peach-400)" below="var(--white)" />
```

Pass the real colours of the sections above and below; the component paints `above` as its band and the curve in `below`. Vary `variant` and `flip` down a page so no two consecutive boundaries look alike. Full-bleed always — never inside the 1200 content column.
