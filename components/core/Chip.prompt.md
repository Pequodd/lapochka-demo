Pill filter chip — white fill, 2px peach-400 border, coral-700 label at 12/600.

```jsx
<Chip selected>все</Chip>
<Chip line="retro" count={3}>ретро</Chip>
<Chip>без кофеина</Chip>
```

Selected chips fill: coral-700 by default, or the product line's own colour when `line` is set. Chips are lowercase; there is no chip larger than 12px anywhere in the system.
